Adolescent program · ages 13–18

A place where teens can heal without putting life on hold.

Our adolescent program is designed for young people ages 13–18 struggling with substance use disorder, with treatment shaped around adolescent development, family needs, and the importance of staying connected to education.

Ages 13–18Dedicated adolescent program for boys and girls.
Structured settingSupervised care with a consistent daily routine.
Clinical supportTherapy, nursing and medication management as indicated.
Family involvementFamily therapy is part of the treatment approach.
What families can expect
  • Individual therapy and daily therapeutic groups
  • Family therapy and behavioral health treatment
  • Nursing and medical oversight, including medication management when indicated
  • Medical detox when clinically appropriate
  • Educational support so school remains part of the conversation
