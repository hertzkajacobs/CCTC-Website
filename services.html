01 · Medical Detox

Start safely, with support.

Medical detox can be the first step for someone who needs support while withdrawing from alcohol or other substances. Cherry Creek provides nursing and medical support and helps plan the next step in treatment.

  • 24/7 nursing support
  • Medical oversight and medication support when appropriate
  • Comfort-focused care and nutritious meals
  • Transition planning into ongoing treatment
Ask About Detox →
02 · Residential / Inpatient

Space to focus on recovery.

Residential treatment provides a structured environment where clients can focus on recovery away from the pressures and triggers of everyday life.

  • Structured daily treatment
  • Individual and group therapy
  • Family involvement and support
  • Medical and clinical team support
Ask About Residential →
03 · Partial Hospitalization

More structure, with more independence.

PHP provides intensive daytime treatment for people who need significant support but may not require 24/7 residential care.

  • Intensive daytime treatment
  • Clinical and therapeutic support
  • Structured recovery planning
  • Connection to the next appropriate level of care
Ask About PHP →
04 · Intensive Outpatient

Keep moving forward with support.

IOP offers structured treatment while allowing more flexibility for work, school, family, and daily life.

  • Structured therapy and groups
  • Recovery skills and relapse-prevention support
  • Flexible step-down care
  • Continued connection with the treatment team
Ask About IOP →
05 · Outpatient

Support that fits everyday life.

Outpatient care provides continued counseling and recovery support while a person lives at home and manages normal responsibilities.

  • Ongoing counseling
  • Peer and recovery support
  • Continued treatment planning
  • Support as needs change
